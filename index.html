About George

George Kiti Jambo is a Kenyan student pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Agricultural Education and Extension at Egerton University. His interests centre on agriculture, education, knowledge sharing and the role of practical information in strengthening farming communities.

Through his learning and community-focused interests, he aims to contribute to agricultural awareness and the sharing of useful farming information.

Founder of PwaniAgritalks

George is the founder of PwaniAgritalks, an agricultural information initiative created to make practical farming knowledge more accessible, with a focus on farmers in Kenya's Coastal region. The platform covers topics including crop production, livestock keeping and sustainable agriculture, and provides a way for farmers to ask questions.
