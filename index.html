A little about me

Learning today.
Contributing tomorrow.

George Kiti Jambo is a Kenyan student pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Agricultural Education and Extension at Egerton University.

His interests centre on agriculture, education, knowledge sharing and the role of practical information in strengthening farming communities. He is particularly interested in making useful agricultural knowledge more accessible to farmers.

“Agricultural knowledge makes the greatest difference when it reaches the people who need it.”

My initiative

Founder of PwaniAgritalks

George is the founder of PwaniAgritalks, an agricultural information initiative focused on making practical farming knowledge more accessible, especially to farmers in Kenya's Coastal region.

The platform shares information on crop production, livestock keeping and sustainable agriculture, and gives farmers a way to ask questions.
